## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a clinical, authoritative, yet deeply humane and welcoming presence for higher academic nursing and healthcare education. It balances rigorous institutional credentials with approachable warmth to serve prospective student nurses, clinical faculty, affiliated hospital administrators, and the families of patients in geriatric outreach programs.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Corporate / Modern Institutional Healthcare**:
- **Structured Credibility:** Crisp grids, structured information blocks, clear accreditation signifiers, and academic clarity.
- **Clinical Vitality:** Dominated by deep forest green and fresh emerald tones reminiscent of clinical scrubs, healing botanical heritage, and life sciences.
- **Human Warmth & Action:** Balanced by high-energy warm amber and fiery orange accents, reflecting compassion, urgency, and dedicated human service.
- **Clarity over Clutter:** White and pristine off-white slate canvases prevent visual fatigue, ensuring complex academic curriculum details, admission deadlines, and clinical facility disclosures remain effortlessly legible.

## Colors

The color palette translates the visual legacy of clinical nursing education into a refined, accessible UI palette built on high functional contrast.

- **Primary (`#055928` / `#0e7039`):** Forest Green commands authority. It anchors top-level institutional headers, primary buttons, hero banners, and foundational credential blocks.
- **Secondary (`#15803d`):** Emerald Green acts as an active clinical complement, used for secondary interaction states, program specialty chips, healthcare service icons, and supportive callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#ea580c` / `#f97316`):** Warm Amber/Orange provides high-priority urgency and action orientation. It highlights application deadlines, "Admissions Open" flags, primary application CTA buttons, and notification triggers.
- **Neutral (`#0f172a`):** Deep Slate neutralizes harsh pure black, providing ink-like readability for headings and body typography across light environments.
- **Backgrounds (`#f8fafc` & `#ffffff`):** Layered neutral grounds create breathable, sanitary, and organized visual divisions between distinct departments and programs. Tinted accents like `#f0fdf4` soften testimonial and clinical service sections.

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers and navigational elements with **Inter** for dense, readable institutional body copy.

- **Headlines & Signage (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern geometric contours with humane humanist curves. Its strong terminal weights lend authority to programme names, provost messages, and institutional statistics, avoiding sterile clinical coldness.
- **Body & Data Content (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and engineered for data-dense layouts, admissions eligibility lists, course descriptions, and contact matrices.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines utilize subtle negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.025em`) for modern density and impact, while small uppercase category badges and overlines expand to positive tracking (`+0.05em`) to ensure instant legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt rhythmic grid within a 12-column responsive layout system that adapts to complex academic portals:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with a max container width of 1280px, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) outer margins. Multi-column cards display 3-up for programs and facilities, or 4-up for clinical service grids.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Content organizes cleanly into 2-column card structures.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px margins. Cards reflow strictly into full-width vertical stacks with sticky utility banners.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Section padding follows an expansive institutional rhythm (`space-3xl` / 96px on desktop, scaling down to `space-xl` / 40px on mobile) to give medical facilities, nursing cohorts, and provost statements clear visual distinction.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **tonal surfaces and subtle, atmospheric ambient shadows**, reflecting pristine clinical precision.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Used for base page backgrounds (`#f8fafc`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Elevation):** Surfaces (`#ffffff`) separated by razor-thin borders (`1px solid #e2e8f0`) with an ambient drop shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover States):** Applied when hovering over program cards, admission forms, and facility previews: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(5, 89, 40, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, slightly shifting the card 2px upward.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Navigation Overlays):** Used for mega menus, mobile drawer navigation, and clinical inquiry modals: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` accompanied by a backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`).
- **Dark Inversion Surfaces:** Clinical outreach sections and footer tiers employ deep forest green (`#033b1a` to `#055928`) with zero elevation, relying entirely on inner borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`) to organize content modules.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced **Rounded (`roundedness: 2`)** profile. 

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, badges) use an 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius, projecting precision, approachability, and structural reliability.
- Larger institutional cards, video preview containers, and prominent banner modules utilize `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`) to soften imagery of campus grounds, laboratories, and student cohorts.
- High-priority interactive indicators, such as "HOT" badges, admission status chips, and avatar containers, use pill shapes (`rounded-full`) for instant visual distinction against linear grid blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Admissions/Enrollment):** Filled Warm Orange (`#ea580c`), hover state `#c2410c`, text `#ffffff`. Uses `label-lg`, 8px border radius, 12px 24px padding. Conveys urgency and clear next steps.
- **Institutional Primary:** Filled Forest Green (`#055928`), hover state `#0e7039`, text `#ffffff`. Used for portal logins, prospectus downloads, and primary navigation links.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Surface white with a 1.5px border in `#055928`, text `#055928`, hover background `#f0fdf4`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#055928`, hover background `#e2e8f0` with 8px radius.

### Cards
- **Academic Programme Cards:** White card background with a subtle border (`#e2e8f0`), top photographic ratio (16:9), absolute positioned pill badges (e.g., "HOT", "3 Years Full-Time"), bold title in `#0f172a`, concise program summary, and a bottom pinned orange/green CTA button.
- **Clinical Service Modules:** Inverted deep forest green surface (`#055928` with subtle border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), centering an outlined emerald badge icon, white headline, and soft slate-green body text.
- **Testimonial & Provost Feature:** Soft horizontal container with high-contrast portrait placement, subtle quotation styling, rating stars in `#f97316`, and institutional verification markers.

### Badges & Chips
- **Status & Category Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), 4px 12px padding, `label-sm` uppercase.
  - *Urgent/Hot:* Background `#ffedd5`, text `#c2410c`.
  - *Accredited/Active:* Background `#dcfce7`, text `#15803d`.
  - *Departmental Tag:* Background `#f1f5f9`, text `#334155`.

### Form Fields & Enquiry Bar
- **Header Enquiry Strip:** High-efficiency horizontal container in `#033b1a`, containing rounded inputs with `#ffffff` backgrounds, subtle borders, crisp placeholder text, and a direct action trigger button.
- **Input Fields:** 44px height for touch compliance, 8px radius, border `#cbd5e1`, focus border `#0e7039` with an emerald focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 112, 57, 0.2)`).

### Navigation & Institutional Bar
- **Top Utility Strip:** Compact dark slate or forest bar displaying emergency lines, contact emails, and student portal shortcuts.
- **Main Header:** Sticky white surface with slight backdrop blur, showcasing the institutional crest and title on the left, clear categorized dropdown links (About, Admission, Virtual Library, Network), and an orange "Apply Now" button on the far right.
