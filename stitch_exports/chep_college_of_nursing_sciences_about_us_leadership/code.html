Institutional Genesis & Legitimacy

A Trajectory of Uncompromising Standards

Established over six years ago along Agbebi Estate Road in Ota, CHEP College of Nursing Sciences was founded to resolve critical staffing deficits in tertiary health centers across Ogun State and neighboring Lagos metropolis.

assignment_turned_in

Accredited by NMCN

Full programmatic accreditation by the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria (NMCN). Curricula fulfill all national standards for Basic Nursing, Post-Basic Specialization, and university-affiliated degree progression.

Year 1 • Inception & Charter

Foundation of the Ota Academic Enclave

Campus lands secured at No. 18, Agbebi Estate Road, Honda Bus-Stop. Initial anatomical demonstration suites and pre-clinical demonstration wards completed.

Year 3 • Regulatory Breakthrough

NMCN Verification & Licensure Charter

Inspection committees from the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria ratify our high-fidelity skills lab and classroom infrastructure, authorizing the matriculation of full-time cohorts.

Year 5 • Clinical Expansion

Specialized Geriatric Care Division Launch

CHEP pioneers 24/7 Geriatric Assessment Units and home-care rotations, filling an overlooked medical gap in elderly cognitive, arthritic, and chronic disease management across Ogun State.

Present & Forward • Digital Horizon

CHEP Virtual Library & 242+ Alumni Worldwide

Fully digital OPAC catalog, research repositories, and simulation-based curriculum driving 100% exam pass outcomes for current candidates.

Welcome to CHEP College of Nursing Sciences, where excellence meets compassion in the heart of healthcare education. We proudly nurture students into skilled, ethical, and confident professionals ready to transform lives through quality nursing practice.

To our dear students, esteemed parents, and passionate advocates of healthcare reform, CHEP is your institution of first choice. In an era where global medicine demands both high-tech agility and timeless human dignity, our collegiate curriculum delivers intensive bedside exposure from Year One.

Under our administration, every candidate masters emergency trauma response, pediatric care, maternal midwifery, and our trademark specialization in comprehensive geriatric support. Our graduates serve across Nigeria's apex teaching hospitals, as well as the United Kingdom and North America.
