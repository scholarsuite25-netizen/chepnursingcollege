1. STATUTORY GOVERNANCE & REGULATORY LEGAL AUTHORITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria (NMCN) Act, Cap N143 Laws of the
Federation of Nigeria (LFN) 2004 empowers the Council to determine the standards
of clinical competence and knowledge required for professional practice.
Key Statutory Examination Directives:
- Curricular Proportionality: Clinical exams must cover the 6 core nursing domains:
  Medical-Surgical, Maternal/Midwifery, Pediatrics, Psychiatry, Public Health, and Trauma/Emergency.
- 50.0% Pass Mark: Strictly enforced across each clinical station and aggregate paper.
- Distinction Threshold: >= 75.0% aggregate score without critical safety violations.
- Critical Patient Safety Policy: Commission or omission of any predefined fatal error
  (e.g., omitting twin palpation prior to oxytocics, head-tilt in cervical trauma)
  triggers an immediate station referral regardless of accumulated checklist points.

STATION 1: Medical-Surgical Nursing (Adult Health)
- Scenario: Post-Op Day 4 laparotomy, wound infection with purulent drainage, central line in situ.
- Vitals: BP 138/86, HR 104, RR 22, Temp 38.6 C, SpO2 97%, Pain 7/10.
- Key Actions: Identity check, hand hygiene, Levine swab culture, 2% chlorhexidine CVC site prep (30s air dry), sterile Tegaderm dressing, yellow biohazard waste disposal.
- 10 MCQs: Levine technique, chlorhexidine drying time, qSOFA sepsis criteria, proliferative healing, evisceration protocol, aminoglycoside TDM, CVC dressing intervals, surgical stroke direction, lactate >2.0 mmol/L, biohazard waste codes.

STATION 2: Maternal & Obstetric Nursing (Midwifery)
- Scenario: Immediate post-delivery of 3.4 kg infant.
- Vitals: BP 116/74, HR 82, RR 18, Temp 36.8 C, SpO2 99%.
- Key Actions: Palpate to rule out second twin, Oxytocin 10 IU IM within 1 min, Brandt-Andrews counter-traction during contraction, placental delivery and rotation, immediate fundal massage, cotyledon inspection.
- 10 MCQs: Second twin exclusion rationale, Oxytocin first-line dosing, placental detachment signs, uterine inversion mechanism, 4 T's of PPH, Ergometrine contraindications in hypertension, retained cotyledon complications, WHO PPH definition (>500 mL), 4th stage monitoring (q15m for 1h), early suckling oxytocin reflex.

STATION 3: Pediatric & Neonatal Nursing
- Scenario: Depressed term neonate, thick meconium, limp, cyanotic, apneic, HR 45 bpm.
- Vitals: HR 45, RR 0, Temp 35.5 C, SpO2 58% at 1 min.
- Key Actions: Radiant warmer, sniffing position, mouth before nose suction, PPV with 21% O2 at 40-60 bpm, MR. SOPA troubleshooting, 2-thumb chest compressions at 3:1 ratio, exact Epinephrine calculation (0.6 mL of 1:10,000 for 3 kg infant).
- 10 MCQs: 21% room air vs hyperoxia, suction sequence, PPV rate (40-60 bpm), MR. SOPA steps, compression indication (HR < 60 bpm), 3:1 ratio rationale, Epinephrine math, 2-thumb superiority, pre-ductal SpO2 site (right wrist), 1-min SpO2 norm (60-65%).

STATION 4: Mental Health & Psychiatric Nursing
- Scenario: Agitated 24-year-old student, command auditory hallucinations to commit suicide, clutching broken glass.
- Vitals: BP 144/92, HR 112, RR 24, Temp 37.1 C, SpO2 98%.
- Key Actions: Clear exit path, 45-degree open posture, calm validation of terror without reinforcing delusions, surrender of weapon to table, focused MSE, explicit C-SSRS suicide risk assessment, Level 1 (1:1) continuous observation.
- 10 MCQs: Personal exit safety rules, therapeutic responses to delusions, command hallucination risk, Level 1 1:1 criteria, acute dystonia recognition, Benztropine antidote, NMS symptoms, suicide questioning facts, Lithium window (0.6-1.0 mEq/L), involuntary admission law.

STATION 5: Community & Public Health Nursing
- Scenario: PHC 14-hour power failure, refrigerator +14 C, cholera diarrhea outbreak.
- Vitals: Child in hypotensive shock (BP 76/48, HR 136, skin pinch > 2s).
- Key Actions: Keep lid closed, cold box preparation, VVM Stage 3/4 condemnation, Shake Test protocol, IMCI Severe Dehydration classification, Plan C IV Ringer's Lactate (100 mL/kg), 1-liter ORS preparation, 0.5% chlorine disinfection, IDSR 24h notification.
- 10 MCQs: Standard +2 to +8 C range, VVM discard stages, freeze-sensitive vaccines, Shake Test principle, severe dehydration criteria, Ringer's Lactate rationale, ORS 1-liter volume, 24h ORS expiry, 0.5% chlorine for excreta, IDSR reporting timeline.

STATION 6: Emergency, Trauma & Critical Care Nursing
- Scenario: Unrestrained MVC driver, tracheal shift to left, absent right breath sounds, JVD, BP 72/40.
- Vitals: BP 72/40, HR 142, RR 34, SpO2 74%.
- Key Actions: In-line C-spine stabilization with jaw-thrust, clear oral airway, clinical tension pneumothorax diagnosis without X-ray, needle thoracostomy in 2nd ICS over 3rd rib, 100% O2 via NRB mask, dual large-bore IVs with warmed crystalloids, GCS scoring (12/15), START Red Tag.
- 10 MCQs: Jaw-thrust in trauma, tension pneumothorax triad, danger of delaying decompression for X-ray, needle thoracostomy anatomy, trauma lethal triad, GCS calculation, START Red Tag criteria, Poiseuille's law in IV cannulae, Beck's triad in cardiac tamponade, 5 H's and 5 T's.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. "Checklist Robot" Syndrome: Current exams reward rote memory over genuine adaptive clinical judgment.
2. Human Examiner Inconsistency: Leniency, severity drift, and subconscious bias distort human grading.
3. Inability to Test Clinical Agility: Real patients present with messy comorbidities and conflicting symptoms.
4. Absence of Algorithmic Stewardship: Fails to test whether nurses can safely critique and audit clinical AI recommendations.

The Role of AI Today:
- Natural Language Simulated Patients: LLMs responding organically to verbal de-escalation, empathy, and history taking.
- Automated Objective Rubric Scoring: Immediate evaluation of candidate statements and procedural sequences with zero bias.
- Continuous Psychometric Tuning: Automatic detection of cultural bias and item difficulty drift.

The Role of AI in the Future:
- Generative Adaptive Scenarios: Dynamically synthesizes unique, un-leakable clinical cases on the fly.
- Cognitive Load & Stress Telemetry: Tracks pupil dilation and vocal tremor to assess clinical composure under pressure.
- Algorithmic Stewardship Assessment: Presents nurses with AI-generated EHR advice that the candidate must audit and override if erroneous.
- Multi-Agent Virtual Healthcare Teams: Simulated physician, student nurse, and phlebotomist voice interactions to test SBAR escalation and team delegation.
